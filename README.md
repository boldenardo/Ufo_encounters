## 🌌 What this is

**A living, navigable globe of every major UAP encounter humanity has on record.**

Not a list. Not a database query. A **3D Earth** — atmosphere, starfield, cinematic camera — where every point of light is a real incident, and every click drops you into the case file.

The May 2026 declassification reshaped public discourse overnight. Files that lived for decades inside FBI vaults, NASA archives, and Pentagon command centers are now public. **This project ties them together** — geographically, visually, and with the receipts to back every claim.

---

## ✨ Why it stands out

🌍 **A real globe — not a flat map.** MapLibre's globe projection with atmosphere, starfield, and orbital lighting. Auto-rotates when you let it breathe.

🎯 **Cinematic fly-to.** Click any encounter and the camera tilts, zooms, and lands on the exact coordinates. Like Google Earth, but the destination is *unsettling*.

🛰️ **Two views per case.** Switch between **satellite imagery** and a **GeoGuessr-style 360° Street View**. Stand where the witnesses stood.

🔥 **Heatmap → pinpoints.** Zoom out to see hotspots burn across continents. Zoom in for individual reports. The visual story rewrites itself with the camera.

📚 **Every claim cited.** Each case links to:
- The original **declassified FOIA document** (Black Vault, FBI Vault, archives.gov, UK MoD, GEPAN/CNES)
- **Wikipedia** for the cultural narrative
- **Peer-reviewed analysis** when it exists (Condon Report, Sheffield Hallam, AIAA, NCBI)
- **AARO / Pentagon references** — the May 2026 Historical Record cross-references

🤖 **Ask the archive anything.** A built-in AI assistant answers questions grounded in the dataset only. No hallucinations about cases that don't exist.

🔴 **Live community pulse.** Real-time UAP discussion from r/UFOs and Bluesky #UAP, geolocated and pinned to the globe as they happen. The world's UAP awareness, visualized.

📱 **Made for any screen.** Collapse the menu, take in the full Earth. Mobile, tablet, desktop — same cinematic experience.

## 🧬 Inside the archive

**37 verified incidents across 14+ countries**, spanning four eras of human encounter:

### 🇺🇸 The Pentagon Era (2015 → 2026)
The encounters that broke through. **GIMBAL** and **GOFAST** — Navy F/A-18 FLIR captures from the USS Theodore Roosevelt strike group. The **2023 Western US "orbs launching orbs"** — described by the Pentagon itself as *"among the most compelling within AARO's current holdings."* All sourced from the May 2026 PURSUE drop.

### 🌎 The International Catalog
Cases that never made US headlines but shook other governments:
- 🇧🇷 **Trindade Island** (1958) — endorsed by President Kubitschek
- 🇧🇷 **Operação Prato** (1977) — Brazilian Air Force investigation, Amazonian villagers reporting burns from "chupa-chupa" lights
- 🇧🇷 **Varginha** (1996) — three witnesses, alleged military recovery, files partially declassified in 2010
- 🇮🇷 **Tehran F-4** (1976) — DIA report distributed to White House, NSA, CIA. Both fighters' weapons systems failed during pursuit
- 🇿🇼 **Ariel School** (1994) — 62 children, identical drawings, Harvard psychiatrist John Mack interviews
- 🇫🇷 **Trans-en-Provence** (1981) — only physical-trace UFO case ever filed by a national space agency (CNES/GEPAN)
- 🇬🇧 **Calvine** (1990) — diamond-shaped craft photographed alongside RAF Harrier. Hidden in MoD vaults for 32 years.

### 📸 The Visual Evidence Cases
Encounters where the witnesses brought back proof:
- **McMinnville/Trent farm** (1950) — disc photographed by Oregon farmer, analyzed by the Air Force-funded Condon Committee
- **Mariana UFO Film** (1950) — earliest known motion-picture UFO footage. Project Blue Book classified as unexplained.
- **Lubbock Lights** (1951) — Texas Tech freshman's photographs, reprinted by Life magazine, never resolved.
- **Falcon Lake** (1967) — geologist Stephen Michalak's burn marks, RCMP and RCAF investigation.

### 🛰️ The Space Records
Even orbit isn't outside the archive. **Skylab 3** (1973) — NASA crew photographs of a reddish rotating object during EVA over the Indian Ocean.

## 🔭 The mission

Most UAP archives live in three places: dusty PDFs nobody reads, paywalled databases, and Reddit threads that vanish. **This project pulls them into one cinematic, open, citable surface** — built so anyone can verify, contribute, and explore.

When the May 2026 disclosure happened, the public had nowhere coherent to go. **Now it does.**

---

## 🧱 Built on

Modern open-source foundations: **MapLibre GL** for the 3D globe (the open fork that left Mapbox proprietary licensing behind), **React + TypeScript + Vite** for the experience, **Tailwind v4** for the design language, and **Vercel** for the planet-scale hosting. The AI assistant runs on **Groq** for sub-second response times.
